Send a Wire Transfer

Send a domestic or international wire transfer using online or mobile banking.
PREREQUISITE
Have the recipient's bank name, account number, and routing (or SWIFT) number ready before you begin.
  1. Log in to online or mobile banking.
  2. Select Transfers & Payments, then Wire Transfer.
  3. Choose Domestic or International.
  4. Enter the recipient and bank details, then the transfer amount.
  5. Review the fee disclosure and confirm the transfer.
Result
Domestic wires submitted before 4:00 PM ET are typically processed the same business day. For help, call 1-800-555-0199.
