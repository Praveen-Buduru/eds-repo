Know Your Customer (KYC) Requirements

Identity verification standards that apply whenever a new account is opened at Meridian Trust Bank.
Federal law requires financial institutions to obtain, verify, and record information that identifies each person who opens an account. This is part of the Customer Identification Program (CIP) under the USA PATRIOT Act.

Required Documentation

Before an account can be activated, customers must provide:
  • A government-issued photo ID (passport, driver's license, or state ID)
  • A Social Security Number or Individual Taxpayer Identification Number
  • A current residential address (P.O. boxes are not accepted)
  • Date of birth confirming the applicant meets the minimum account age

Verification Process

  1. Documents are captured at account opening (branch, app, or web).
  2. Identity is verified electronically against government and credit bureau databases.
  3. If verification cannot be completed electronically, the customer is asked to submit documents for manual review within 10 business days.
  4. Accounts that fail verification are restricted to deposit-only status until resolved.
