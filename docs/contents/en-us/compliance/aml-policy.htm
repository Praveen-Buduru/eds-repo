Anti-Money Laundering (AML) Policy.

Overview of related obligations for Meridian Trust Bank staff and systems.
AML
Anti-Money Laundering. Laws, regulations, and procedures designed to prevent the practice of generating income through illegal actions.
Meridian Trust Bank maintains an AML program consistent with the Bank Secrecy Act and applicable FinCEN regulations. The program includes customer due diligence, ongoing transaction monitoring, and mandatory reporting of suspicious activity.

Transaction Monitoring

Automated systems flag transactions that fall outside expected patterns, including large cash deposits, rapid movement of funds between accounts, and transfers to high-risk jurisdictions.

Reporting Obligations

Suspicious Activity Reports (SARs) and Currency Transaction Reports (CTRs) are filed in accordance with federal timelines. Employees must escalate any suspected activity to the Compliance team immediately.
