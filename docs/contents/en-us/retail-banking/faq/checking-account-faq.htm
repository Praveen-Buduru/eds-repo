Checking Account FAQ

Common questions about Meridian Advantage Checking.
Is there a monthly fee?
The monthly fee is waived with a qualifying direct deposit or a minimum daily balance. See Current Rates & Fees for details.
Overdraft
A transaction that exceeds the available balance in an account, which may incur a fee unless overdraft protection is enabled.
How do I contact support?
For assistance, contact Meridian Trust Bank Customer Service at 1-800-555-0199, available Monday-Friday, 7am-9pm ET.
