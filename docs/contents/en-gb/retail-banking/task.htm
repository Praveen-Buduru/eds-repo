Close a Retail Account

Steps to close a checking or savings account in good standing.
PREREQUISITE
Ensure the account balance is zero and all pending transactions have cleared before closing.
  1. Contact Meridian Trust Bank at 1-800-555-0199 or visit a branch.
  2. Verify your identity with the representative.
  3. Request final disbursement of any remaining balance by check or transfer.
  4. Confirm closure and request written confirmation for your records.
Result
Closed accounts are reflected on your next statement.
